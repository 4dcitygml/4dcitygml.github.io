01

The idea

Treat change as part of the model.

Keep source-compatible CityGML in Git, preserve stable identifiers, and make each reviewed building change traceable. Format migrations become reproducible releases—not breaks in history.

03

A small, inspectable workflow

Source. Review. Release.

  1. 01

    Keep the source edition

    The repository starts from a documented public dataset and keeps its source-compatible form as the canonical record.

  2. 02

    Review by building

    Stable IDs, focused commits, evidence, and automated checks make individual changes easier to inspect.

  3. 03

    Generate other editions

    Validated conversions are published as immutable derived releases with manifests and checksums.
